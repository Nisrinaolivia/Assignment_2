An HR officer at Coastline Logistics (a national freight company with 900 employees) left a work laptop on a tram at about 5:30pm. The laptop held a spreadsheet of employee names, bank account details and salaries. The laptop's hard drive is fully encrypted with BitLocker and requires a strong password, which was not written down anywhere. The IT team remotely locked and wiped the device at 6:00pm, and logs confirm the wipe was completed and that no one logged in before then. The laptop was handed in to Yarra Trams lost property the next morning.
