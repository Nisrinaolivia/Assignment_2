Harbour Accounting is a small accounting practice in Geelong with five staff and an annual turnover of about $1.6 million. It does not provide health services or trade in personal information. On 16 October 2026, a staff member accidentally emailed a client's completed tax return to the wrong person: another client with a similar surname. The tax return included the client's name, home address, tax file number, income details and bank account details. About two hours later, the staff member noticed the error and emailed the recipient asking them to delete it. The recipient replied "No worries, deleted", but Harbour Accounting has no way to verify this and has no contract with the recipient covering confidentiality. The recipient is a long-standing client with no known issues.
