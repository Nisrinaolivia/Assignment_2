Brightside Dental is a chain of 12 dental clinics in Victoria with annual turnover of about $8 million. On 3 October, an attacker exploited an unpatched vulnerability in its patient booking system and downloaded a database containing names, dates of birth, home addresses, phone numbers, Medicare numbers and treatment notes for around 40,000 patients. The data was not encrypted. On 10 October, a sample of the data was posted on a dark web forum with an offer to sell the full set. Brightside has patched the system but has not been able to get the data removed.
