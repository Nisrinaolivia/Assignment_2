Brightside Dental Pty Ltd operates 12 dental clinics in Victoria and has an annual turnover of about $8 million. On 3 October 2026, an attacker exploited an unpatched vulnerability in its online patient booking system and downloaded a database containing the names, dates of birth, home addresses, phone numbers, Medicare numbers and dental treatment notes of around 40,000 patients. The database was not encrypted. Brightside discovered the attack on 9 October 2026 when a sample of the data was posted on a dark web forum with an offer to sell the full set. Brightside has since patched the system and reset all staff passwords, but it has not been able to get the data removed from the forum.
