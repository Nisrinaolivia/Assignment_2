Coastline Energy is an electricity retailer with around 60,000 customers and an annual turnover of about $45 million. On 14 October 2026 at about 5:30pm, a customer service manager left a work laptop on a Melbourne tram. The laptop contained a spreadsheet of 1,200 customers' names, home addresses and direct-debit bank account details. The laptop's hard drive is fully encrypted with BitLocker (AES-256) and protected by a strong password that was not written down or stored with the device. The manager reported the loss at 5:50pm. The IT team remotely locked and wiped the laptop at 6:00pm, and the device management logs confirm that the wipe completed and that nobody logged in between the loss and the wipe. The laptop was handed in to Yarra Trams lost property the next morning.
